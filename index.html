What Happens Next

1. You email us what you have and where it is. 2. We reply with a pickup plan and a time. 3. At pickup, we count and log every device in front of you. 4. We wipe or destroy the drives and send you one certificate per drive, listed by serial number. If the plan does not suit you, say so and we stop there.

Questions

What does it cost? Local pickup and certified destruction are free for standard lots. We keep the resale value of the hardware; that is the whole deal. Unusual lots (heavy CRT volume, oversized items) are quoted line by line first.

How do I know my data is gone? Every drive gets a certificate with its serial number, the method used, and the validator's signature, following the NIST Rev. 2 Sec. 4.6 field list.

What will you not take? Printers, microwaves, large appliances over 30 lbs, air conditioners, floor speakers, large CRT screens. Ink and alkaline batteries must be removed before pickup; we cannot handle them.
