Questions

What does it cost?
Local pickup and certified destruction are free for standard lots. We keep the resale value of the hardware; that is the whole deal. Unusual lots (heavy CRT volume, oversized items) are quoted line by line first.

How do I know my data is gone?
Every drive gets a certificate with its serial number, the method used, and the validator's signature, following the NIST Rev. 2 Sec. 4.6 field list.

What will you not take?
Printers, microwaves, large appliances over 30 lbs, air conditioners, floor speakers, large CRT screens. Ink and alkaline batteries must be removed before pickup; we cannot handle them.
