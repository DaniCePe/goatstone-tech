Remarketing

Recovered units are graded and listed through resale channels, one at a time and in wholesale lots. Each unit ships with its sanitization record, tied to its serial number.

For buyers

Wholesale lots of business-class desktops and laptops are the standard offer; lot composition, grades, and per-unit pricing are published with each lot. Every drive in every unit has been purged or replaced and certified. Ask for the current lot list.

For sellers

Remarketing is where liquidation value comes from. Equipment with resale value offsets free pickup and certified destruction on the rest of your lot; equipment without it is recycled downstream at no landfill cost to you.
