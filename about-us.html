About us

A cybersecurity startup doing IT asset disposition from West Chicago, Illinois.

Who we are

Goatstone Tech exists for one problem: retired computers, drives, and phones still carry your data long after you stop using them. Throwing them in a dumpster does not retire the risk, and most small organizations have no IT department to handle it.

We are a startup, and we say so. We run lean, we pick up locally for free, and we do the security step properly: sanitization following NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2, a written policy, verification and validation on every job, and a certificate that names the drive by serial number.

How we work

Document, test, wipe, refurbish, resell. Equipment that comes in is logged at intake, tested, and sanitized. Working equipment returns to service. What cannot be resold goes to named downstream recycling partners; we do not de-manufacture or break down hazardous material ourselves, and nothing we collect goes to landfill.

What we will never claim

NIST does not certify companies or software, and neither do we claim otherwise. Our compliance is self-attested and shown through our policy, our job log, and the per-drive certificate. Read the details on our data destruction page.
