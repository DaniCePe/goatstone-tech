About Us

Retired computers, drives, and phones still hold your data. Most small organizations have nobody assigned to deal with that, so the machines sit in a closet or go out with the trash, data intact. That gap is the work we take.

We are a startup. Pickups are local and free. The data work follows NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2 under a written policy, with verification and validation on every job and a certificate naming each drive by serial number.

How we work

Equipment is logged at intake, tested, and sanitized. Working units go back out for resale. Units with no resale value go to named downstream recycling partners, with receipts. We do not break down hazardous material ourselves. Nothing we collect goes to landfill.

About certification

NIST does not certify companies or software. When we say our sanitization follows NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2, the evidence is the written policy, the job log, and the certificate for each drive. The details are on our data destruction page.
