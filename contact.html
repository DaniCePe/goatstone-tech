What to include

A rough count of devices, what they are (laptops, desktops, loose drives), whether any drives must be destroyed rather than wiped, and your pickup address. Photos help.

We accept

Desktops, laptops, phones, tablets, MP3 players, game consoles, keyboards, mice, monitors, SD cards, HDDs, SSDs, USB drives, cables and chargers, small CRT screens.

We cannot accept

Printers, microwaves, stoves, fridges, freezers, large appliances (over 30 lbs), air conditioners, floor speakers, large CRT screens (over 30 lbs). Please remove ink and alkaline batteries before pickup; we cannot handle them.
