IT Liquidation

Offboarding a site, a refresh cycle, or a closet of retired machines? We take whole lots, log every device, sanitize every drive, and certify each one by serial.

What we take

Desktops, laptops, servers, phones, tablets, monitors, networking gear, and loose drives (HDDs, SSDs, NVMe, USB, SD cards). At intake we count and log each item and record where it is headed before it leaves your site.

How a liquidation runs

1. You send a list or photos of what is going. 2. We quote the pickup (local pickup is free for standard lots). 3. At pickup, every device is logged with make, model, and serial where present. 4. Drives are sanitized to NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2 and certified by serial. 5. Equipment is tested and routed to remarketing, parts, or downstream recycling.

What it costs

For most lots, removal plus certified data destruction is the offer: no charge, and we keep the resale value of the hardware. Large or unusual lots are quoted line by line first.

Questions

Can you sign our paperwork? Yes. We work from a written sanitization policy and a chain-of-custody log, and we sign vendor and asset-release paperwork at pickup.

Do you take servers and networking gear? Yes, including loose drives pulled from arrays; each drive is certified individually.
