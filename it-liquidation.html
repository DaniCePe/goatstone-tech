What happens next

1. You email us what you have and where it is. 2. We reply with a pickup plan and a time. 3. At pickup, every device is counted and logged in front of you. 4. Drives are wiped or destroyed, and you get a certificate per drive, by serial number. No forms with twelve fields, no obligation: if the plan does not work for you, nothing happens.

What we take

Desktops, laptops, servers, phones, tablets, monitors, networking gear, and loose drives (HDDs, SSDs, NVMe, USB, SD cards). One standardized intake: everything is counted, logged, and assigned a destination before it leaves your site.

How a liquidation runs

1. You send a list or photos of what is going. 2. We quote the pickup (local pickup is free for standard lots). 3. At pickup, every device is logged with make, model, and serial where present. 4. Drives are sanitized to NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2 and certified by serial. 5. Equipment is tested and routed to remarketing, parts, or downstream recycling.

What it costs

For most lots, removal plus certified data destruction is the offer: no charge, and we keep the resale value of the hardware. Large or unusual lots are quoted line by line first.

Questions

Can you sign our paperwork?

Do you take servers and networking gear?
Yes, including loose drives pulled from arrays; each drive is certified individually.
