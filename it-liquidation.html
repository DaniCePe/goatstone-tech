IT Liquidation

Whole-lot removal of retired IT equipment for offices, warehouses, schools, and clinics in the western Chicago suburbs.

What we take

Desktops, laptops, servers, phones, tablets, monitors, networking gear, and loose drives (HDDs, SSDs, NVMe, USB, SD cards). One standardized intake: everything is counted, logged, and assigned a destination before it leaves your site.

How a liquidation runs

1. You send a list or photos of what is going. 2. We quote the pickup (local pickup is free for standard lots). 3. At pickup, every device is logged with make, model, and serial where present. 4. Drives are sanitized to NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2 and certified by serial. 5. Equipment is tested and routed to remarketing, parts, or downstream recycling.

What it costs

For most lots, removal plus certified data destruction is the offer: no charge, and we keep the resale value of the hardware. Large or unusual lots (heavy CRT volume, oversized appliances, pallet freight) are quoted line by line first.
