The reuse loop

Every unit is documented at intake, tested (power, screen, battery operation, storage health), sanitized to NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2, cleaned, and prepared for resale. Units that fail testing are harvested for parts where sensible; the rest goes to downstream recycling with a receipt.

What recovers well

Business-class laptops and desktops (Dell Latitude, Lenovo ThinkPad, HP EliteBook class), recent phones and tablets, and working monitors. Consumer gear is taken for recycling; recovery value concentrates in business hardware.

Why it matters

A working machine has resale value; a shredded one has scrap value. Reuse pays better and wastes less. The certificate chain shows the data was sanitized before the unit went back out.
